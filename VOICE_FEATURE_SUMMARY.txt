   BEFORE: Silent processing, no feedback to user

   BEFORE: Manual form filling (4 fields) + manual search click (5+ steps)
   AFTER:  Everything auto-fills & auto-searches (zero manual steps!)
   HOW:    parseVoiceCommand() → form fill → map update → search click
